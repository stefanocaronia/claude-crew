name: tidy
description: Tidy the todos and compact the memories of some colleagues, or of everyone whose todo is past 15 open lines: each, as a short task, ticks what is already done, merges duplicates, keeps the 15 most important open lines and archives the rest. The Crew panel's tidy button runs it; the user may type it too.
argument-hint: "[first names]"
disable-model-invocation: true
---
With first names, those colleagues; without, everyone whose `todo.md` has more than 15 open lines or whose `MEMORY.md` is past 80 lines or 10 KB. Say in one line who, with how many open lines each; when the plan's weekly limit is near (Claude Code: the usage the app shows), ask the user before starting, since each one is a task.

Each colleague gets a task ([task.md](../crew/references/task.md)), all at once and in the background, with this brief: check each open line of your `todo.md` against where the project keeps the state of its work, which Claude knows from the project's context and names with paths (its plan, its decisions, its issue tracker, the history of its changes), and tick what is already done; to a colleague who cannot read them, or for a tracker only Claude reaches, Claude gives the excerpts in the brief; then tidy it as the crew skill says under "Always": move the lines ticked `[x]` to `notes/todo-done.md`, the history of what is done, merge duplicates, keep at most 15 open lines, by priority, P1 first, and move the rest, and what no longer matters, to `notes/todo-archive.md` with the date, never deleted. Then, if your `MEMORY.md` is past 80 lines or 10 KB, compact it as the crew skill says: an index of what holds, the oldest dated sections moved whole to `notes/diary-<year>-<month>.md`, nothing deleted. A line others wait for (its id in someone's `after`) stays open, and its id stays. A line without a priority or a date gets them: the priority from the work's state, the date from when the line was written (the history of the todo, when there is one) or today's. Change no other file. Return one line: how many ticked, merged and archived, the memory's lines before and after, and the three open lines that matter most.

When they are back, give the user one line per colleague from their answer.
