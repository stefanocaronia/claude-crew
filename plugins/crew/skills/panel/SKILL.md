---
name: panel
description: Open the Crew panel beside the chat: the team with portraits, roles and open tasks, who is at work, the meeting. Use when the user asks to see the colleagues, the team or their panel.
---
Call the tool `mcp__crew__panel`, then say in one line that the panel is open. Nothing else. Where the tool is missing, the panel is not installed: say so, and that `/plugin install crew@claude-crew` brings it.

`/crew-panel`, the panel's own command, opens and closes it at once, without Claude.
